Discord Presence

Choose what your Discord status shows. Needs the Discord desktop app open; updates every 30 seconds.

01 / Presence content

Turn on the views you want. They rotate each refresh, or PIN one in the rotation preview below so only that one shows.

02 / Custom text

  1. Type what Discord should show in Line 1 and Line 2. Plain text works, for example “Grinding ranked tonight”.
  2. Add live stats: click a placeholder in the list below. It's inserted where your cursor is, and replaced with your real value every refresh.
  3. Turn on “Show custom text on Discord” to add it to the rotation, or press “Pin custom text” to show only your text.
How it looks on Discord
Placeholders (click one to insert it)

03 / Discord extras

Shows the brawler from your last battle as the big picture, with the map as the small badge. Pictures come from Brawlify's public image library; only the picture link goes to Discord. Your API key never leaves this PC.

Adds a “View Brawl Stars stats” button that opens your Brawlify profile. Discord only shows presence buttons to other people, so you won't see it on your own profile.

04 / Rotation preview

The green outline marks what Discord shows right now. Click PIN on a view to show only that one.
