## What's new in 0.2.5

- **Goals:** on the Stats page, set a target for ranked elo or trophies, with an optional deadline. You get a progress bar, your pace over the last two weeks, an estimated finish date, and whether you're on track for the deadline.
- **Overall meta:** at the bottom of Ranked Picks, the top 10 brawlers across every map (or one game mode), ranked by win rate with their pick rate. It uses the same sample as the rest of the page, so press Refresh sample to update it.
