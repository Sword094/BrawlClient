## What's new in 0.2.0

- **Discord Presence page:** pick which pages rotate, write your own text with placeholders like `{brawler}` and `{trophies}`, pin one page, show brawler and map pictures, and preview it live.
- **Stats overview:** streaks, star player rate, ranked record, a daily activity heat map, and a trophy / elo graph with Today, 7 days, 30 days and All time.
- **Elo fix:** elo gain and loss now shows up reliably, even for games played back to back.
- **Uppercase tags** in the search, compare and settings boxes.
- **Automatic updates:** the app now tells you when a new version is out and installs it for you.
